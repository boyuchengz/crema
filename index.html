Crema

Coffee you can sleep on. Crema shows you, in one big number, when to have your last coffee so it’s gone by bedtime.

Get help

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

What does the big time on Today mean?

The latest time you can have your usual drink and still be under 50 mg of caffeine at your bedtime, a level generally considered low enough not to disturb sleep. It moves earlier with every drink you log. After that time passes, it shows when you’ll be clear for sleep instead.

Why does a drink say “not today”?

Having it now would leave more than 50 mg in your system at bedtime. Hold its cup to see by how much, and when you would be under 50 mg instead.

How do I log a drink I had earlier?

Hold its cup on Today, or choose it under Other, then pick when you had it. To change a drink you’ve already logged, open it from Today or the Journal and tap Edit.

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Crema. The Home Screen widget’s cups log a drink with a tap, and it joins your journal the next time you open Crema. The Control Center button is under Add a Control.

What is “Your cutoff” on the Sleep tab?

When your clear nights and your late nights have different last cups, Crema suggests the time halfway between them. It needs at least a few nights of each.

How is caffeine calculated?

From typical amounts for each drink and size, which you can change on any drink. Crema assumes your body clears half of it every 4, 5, or 7 hours, depending on what you chose for “Coffee Lasts” in Settings. These are estimates, not medical advice.

How do I get the last-call reminder?

Turn on Last-Call Reminder in Settings (the gear on the Today tab). Crema sends one notification 30 minutes before the last call for your usual drink, on days when there’s still time.

Can I stop the “How did you sleep?” question?

Yes. Turn off Ask How I Slept in Settings.

Scanning didn’t fill in my drink.

Scanning reads the printed or written text on the cup or receipt. Make sure the label is in focus and well lit. It fills in only empty fields, so you can type or fix anything it missed.

Where did the map and spending go?

Crema focuses on caffeine and sleep, so it no longer records prices or places. Drinks you logged with them keep them: they still show on each drink’s page and in the export.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. In Settings, under Your Data, choose Export as CSV to get a spreadsheet of every drink.

Does Crema sync between devices?

Not yet. Your log is stored only on your iPhone.
